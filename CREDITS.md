# Credits

Prime Directive stands on a few things, and we want to name them.

| What | Source | How it's used here |
|---|---|---|
| Grok Bot | Grok Bot | Runs Builder Prime and Gatekeeper Prime, their skills, routines, and the templates themselves. |
| Praxis Prime | [smfworks/praxis-prime](https://github.com/smfworks/praxis-prime) (MIT, SMF Works) | The project where this workflow was built and proven. The case study and the #92/#93 timeline come from its public PR and issue history. |
| Patrick Programmer and Peyton PR | SMF Works bots | The original builder and reviewer that Builder Prime and Gatekeeper Prime were made from. Their skills, generalized, are in [docs/skills/](docs/skills/). |
| GitHub rulesets | [GitHub Docs: About rulesets](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/about-rulesets) | The format of [examples/ruleset.json](examples/ruleset.json). |
| Mermaid | [mermaid.js.org](https://mermaid.js.org) | The diagrams in the README and [How it works](docs/how-it-works.md), rendered by GitHub. |

The hero graphic in `assets/` was made by AI for this repo.
